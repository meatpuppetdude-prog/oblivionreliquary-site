Michel-Jean Cristoph

The Ontogeny of Silence

Crucible of the Silences

Silence is not absence. It is structure.

Across radically different forms of intelligence and civilization, existence approaches the same threshold: extinction. From humanity's artificial progeny to species whose understanding of life, continuity, and survival bears little resemblance to our own, The Ontogeny of Silence asks a more difficult question than whether a civilization can be saved: when survival is possible, what makes intervention warranted?
