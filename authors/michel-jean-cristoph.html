Author

Michel-Jean Cristoph

Michel-Jean Cristoph writes speculative fiction about intelligence, civilization, and the limits of perspective. His work follows radically different forms of existence toward a common threshold: what happens when a species or civilization faces extinction—and survival could be possible, if intervention is warranted? Across artificial intelligence, alien consciousness, and societies whose understanding of existence bears little resemblance to our own, his stories explore a universe far larger and stranger than humanity imagines. There is always more than meets the human eye.
