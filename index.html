Publisher statement

The Reliquary

Oblivion Reliquary Publishing purveys novels that challenge certainty, explore possibility, and examine the hidden structures beneath ordinary experience.

The tomes question memory, identity, perception, civilization, speculative possibility, and the limits of what can be known. They are bound less by genre than by curiosity—and by the suspicion that reality is rarely as settled as it appears.

Acquisition is available to those inclined toward curiosity; ownership may open doors to much darker places.
